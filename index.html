THE CONCEPT

This motion graphics explainer breaks down the backend of Uber's driver-matching system—answering a deceptively simple question: how does Uber decide who your driver is? Using crisp map animations, clean vector motion, and kinetic typography, the video contrasts the limitations of a sequential Greedy Algorithm with Uber's batch-based H3 matching approach.

It then dives into how machine learning models like Deep ETA (built on Michelangelo AI) weigh live route conditions, historical traffic data, and driver behaviors to minimize average customer wait times across the entire city.
